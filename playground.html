Would Orbit Guard stop this?

Coding agents ask “can I run this?” all day. Orbit Guard answers the safe ones silently, sends the risky ones to your phone, and refuses the catastrophic ones. Type something an agent might run. This page runs entirely in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.
