# Orbit brand kit

## Files

- `orbit-mark.svg` — primary app icon and social avatar.
- `orbit-mark-mono.svg` — one-color production, merchandise, or embossing.
- `orbit-lockup-dark.svg` — horizontal logo for dark backgrounds.
- `orbit-lockup-light.svg` — horizontal logo for light backgrounds.

All files are SVG and may be exported to PNG at any size. Keep the source SVGs as the canonical assets.

## Meaning

The intersecting paths represent multiple personal systems converging around one calm center. The coral satellite is the single item that needs attention; the luminous center represents the owner, not an AI agent.

## Colors

| Name | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Orbit black | `#10130F` | Primary dark surface |
| Signal lime | `#C8F169` | Focus and resolved state |
| Attention coral | `#FF7B67` | Urgent signal only |
| Paper white | `#EDF1E7` | Text on dark surfaces |
| Moss | `#8E9989` | Supporting text |

## Usage

- Keep clear space around the mark equal to the central circle’s diameter.
- Do not recolor individual orbital paths outside the approved monochrome variant.
- Do not rotate, squash, add drop shadows, or place the lockup over noisy imagery.
- Use the mark alone below 180 px wide. Use the horizontal lockup for repository headers, launch pages, and social banners.
- The tagline may be omitted when the final rendered lockup is below 420 px wide.
